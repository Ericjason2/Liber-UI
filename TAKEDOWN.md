# Takedown procedure

Logos distributed by Liber are trademarks of their respective owners. They are
provided only to refer to the brand (technology lists, partner pages), never to
suggest a partnership or endorsement.

## How to request a removal or a fix

If you own a trademark distributed here, or represent its owner, and want it
removed or corrected:

1. Write to **TODO: contact address to be defined in phase 0** with the subject
   `Takedown: <logo slug>`.
2. Include the logo slug (for example `github`), the page URL, and the reason
   (removal, outdated logo, wrong colors, guideline violation).
3. Tell us how to confirm that you represent the brand owner (for example an
   address on the brand's official domain).

## Our commitments

- Acknowledgement within 2 business days.
- Removal or correction published within **7 days** of a valid request, on
  the website, the registry, the CDN and the npm packages.
- Removing a logo is shipped as a **major** version of the npm packages and
  noted in the changelog.
- Removed pages answer with an explicit "removed at the request of the
  trademark owner" notice.

## Scope

This procedure covers logos (trademarks). For code (blocks, components,
themes), fonts or any other content, open an issue on the repository or write
to the same address.
